FEATURES

My Movies

Manually add movies to your collection. Fill in the key details — title, year, director, runtime, genre, description, and poster. Then set the status to Watchlist or Already Watched, rate it, and save the date you watched it.

Profile

Manage your personal information and preferences. See the number of movies you have watched or want to watch. Find out the average rating you have given to your movies and view your personal stats.

Personal Stats

View all your watching stats. See how many movies you've watched in total, track your monthly activity, and analyze your yearly viewing habits.
